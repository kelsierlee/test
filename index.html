Biography

Early Life & Discipline

Jia-Hao Lee, known online as Kelsier Lee, is currently on the path to becoming an aesthetic dentist, training toward a practice built on precision, care, and an eye for facial beauty. The clinical world demands exactly what he has long practiced at the easel: a steady hand, patient observation, and respect for structure.

Alongside his coursework, Lee is a devoted painter and draughtsman, working primarily in oil and graphite. His visual education has been shaped by the portraiture of John Singer Sargent — the fluid, confident brushwork, the restrained yet luminous palette, and the way a single passage of light can carry the weight of a whole composition.

He sees aesthetic dentistry and painting not as opposites but as parallel disciplines: both are, in the end, quiet acts of transformation — reshaping a smile, or revealing a likeness — each bringing order, proportion, and dignity to the human face. It is a pursuit he carries with him like a recurring emblem: the butterfly, always mid-change.
