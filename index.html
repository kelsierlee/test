Biography

Early Life & Discipline

Jia-Hao Lee, known online as Kelsier Lee, is a dental medicine student at Taipei Medical University with a research focus on periodontology — working toward publication, and a specialty license and master's degree in the United States. He currently serves as President of the TMU Dental Society, having previously directed international affairs for the Taiwan Dental Students' Association (TDSA).

Alongside his coursework, Lee paints, draws, and photographs — a visual practice built on composition and form, and carried into the precision his clinical training demands.

He brings the same standard to both: a steady hand, a calm temperament, and close attention to what a face — or a smile — is trying to say.
